SIO Sushi Experience

About this experience

Enjoy an unforgettable dinner at SIO, one of the most exclusive culinary experiences in Colonia. Discover a refined sushi menu crafted with premium ingredients, artistic presentation, and a unique atmosphere. Perfect for a special night, combining gastronomy, design, and attention to detail.

🎥 SIO on Instagram

How it works

  • 🪑 Your table: A table is reserved for you at SIO in Colonia del Sacramento — arrive at your booking time and your seat will be ready.
  • 🍱 4-step tasting menu: The experience includes a four-course degustation menu with seasonal ingredients, precise technique, and presentation that matches the dining room.
  • ⏱️ Take your time: Allow about two hours to enjoy the full menu at a relaxed pace — ideal for a date night, a celebration, or the standout dinner of your trip.

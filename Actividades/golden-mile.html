The Golden Mile – Colonia Bar Tour

3 bars. 3 experiences. One night in Colonia.

About this experience

★★★★★ Google Reviews Trusted by travelers

Discover Colonia after dark on a guided bar tour through three different sides of the city's nightlife.

Your local host takes care of the route and organization while you enjoy each stop.

Start the evening at a local craft brewery with a tasting of locally brewed beers. Continue to a cocktail bar specializing in signature drinks and mixology, and finish the night at a lively local bar with music and entertainment.

Three different stops, three different atmospheres, and one night already planned for you.

Thursday, Friday & Saturday · 20:00

How it works

  • 01 — Craft Beer Start the night at a local craft brewery and enjoy a tasting of 4 craft beers (100 ml each).
  • 02 — Signature Cocktails Continue to a cocktail bar and enjoy 2 signature cocktails of your choice.
  • 03 — Music & Nightlife Finish the evening at a lively local bar with 1 drink of your choice, music and entertainment.
  • Duration The full experience lasts about 3 hours.
  • The atmosphere at the final venue may vary depending on the night, including karaoke, DJs, live performances, music and entertainment.

USD 120 per person

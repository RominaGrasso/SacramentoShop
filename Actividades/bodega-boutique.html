About this experience

Discover a family boutique winery, just 25 minutes from Colonia del Sacramento. Enjoy a unique wine experience with estate-made wines and round-trip transportation included, choosing between a tasting or lunch. You’ll taste wines served only at the winery — they are not sold anywhere else. Live this experience and take home authentic memories.

How it works

  • 🚐 We pick you up in Colonia: Round-trip transportation from Colonia del Sacramento is included.
  • 🍷 Choose your experience: After tapping “Create your experience”, choose Premium Tasting or Full Lunch.
  • ✨ Enjoy the winery: An intimate boutique setting, local wines and an unhurried visit.
  • 🏨 Return to Colonia: We take you back at the end of the experience.

What's included

  • ✔ Round-trip transportation from Colonia del Sacramento
  • ✔ Guided vineyard and winery visit
  • ✔ Choice of Premium Tasting or Full Lunch
  • ✔ Welcome glass and aperitif
  • ✔ Free-flow wine tasting on the lunch option

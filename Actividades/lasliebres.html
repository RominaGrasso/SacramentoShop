About this experience

A private transfer takes you to Comarca Las Liebres. You'll walk through the vineyard and organic garden and enjoy a curated tasting of three Uruguayan wines paired with seasonal dishes from the kitchen. Then a private transfer takes you to a guided horseback ride on the outskirts of Colonia, in beautiful open countryside. Plan on about 3 to 4 hours for the full experience (visit, tasting, ride, and transfers).

🎥 Watch Reel on @_sacramentoadventures

How it works

  • 🚗 Transfer to Las Liebres: At 11:15 a private transfer picks you up and takes you directly to Comarca Las Liebres.
  • 🍇 Vineyard & garden: Guided walk through the vines and organic garden at Las Liebres.
  • 🍷 Wine tasting: Three wines with food pairings — confirm any dietary notes when you create your order.
  • 🐎 Horseback ride: At 13:30 we take you from Las Liebres to the ride on the outskirts of Colonia, near Colonia Soho, through open countryside trails.
  • 🏨 Return: The same private service picks you up when you finish and drops you at your hotel or the terminal.

USD 85 per guest

Private transport is billed by vehicle: USD 50 total for 1–4 guests (one vehicle).

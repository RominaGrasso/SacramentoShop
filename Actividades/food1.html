About this experience

Discover Colonia del Sacramento through a guided experience that blends history, gastronomy, and local culture. Walk through the city’s charming streets with a knowledgeable guide, starting from the iconic Plaza de la Basílica. Along the way, you will learn about Colonia’s Portuguese and Spanish heritage, hidden corners, and local stories. The experience also includes a stop to enjoy an authentic Uruguayan chivito — one of the country’s most iconic dishes.

🎥 Watch Reel on @_sacramentoadventures

How it works

  • 🏛 Meeting point: Plaza de la Basílica (Historic Quarter).
  • 🚶 Guided walking tour: Explore Colonia’s historic streets, learn about its past, and discover hidden gems with a local guide. Then the guide accompanies you to the restaurant and continues their route.
  • 🍽 Lunch experience: Enjoy an authentic Uruguayan chivito at a local restaurant.
  • More meal options are available — milanesa and tourist menu (you can see them when you create your menu).

USD 50 per person · Duration: ~3 hours

What's included

  • ✔ Guided walking tour with local expert
  • ✔ Authentic Uruguayan chivito
  • ✔ French fries or fresh salad

What's not included

  • ✗ Drinks and desserts (you can order them on site)

About Hotel Royal

Hotel Royal is located on one of Colonia del Sacramento's main avenues, just 200 meters from the UNESCO Historic Quarter (World Heritage Site since 1995). The beach is also 200 meters away, and the river and land terminals are eight blocks from the hotel. Enjoy breakfast included, a swimming pool, and comfortable single, double, and triple rooms — an ideal base to explore Colonia.

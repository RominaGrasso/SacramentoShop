About this experience

Enjoy an exclusive sunset boat experience along the river with Your boat captain, designed for corporate groups and private events. Relax on a private boat while the sun sets over the water, creating a unique and unforgettable atmosphere.

After the navigation, continue the evening with a premium dinner at a partner restaurant, where you will experience a refined and intimate dining setting.

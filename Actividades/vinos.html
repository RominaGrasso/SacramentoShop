About this experience

Discover a unique wine experience at the wine shop, a charming wine shop in the heart of Colonia del Sacramento's Historic Quarter. Surrounded by cobblestone streets and colonial architecture, taste selected Uruguayan wines in a cozy, authentic setting — the perfect complement after a guided tour.

🎥 Watch Reel on @_sacramentoadventures

How it works

  • 📅 Book your tasting: Choose your visit date and tasting option — add one order per guest.
  • 🕐 Opening hours: Open Monday to Friday, from 11 AM to 7 PM.
  • 🏛 Arrive at the wine shop: Meet us in Colonia's Historic Quarter at the agreed time.
  • 🍷 Enjoy your pairing: Savor Uruguayan wines, with empanadas on the options that include them.
  • 🏷 Shop with a bonus: With this experience, you get 10% off in-store purchases at the wine shop.

From USD 30 per guest · Mon–Fri · 11 AM – 7 PM

Three tasting options at the wine shop (USD 30, USD 35, or USD 40). Uruguayan wines. Options 1 and 2 include three empanadas. Add one order per guest — the summary and total update automatically.

What's included

  • ✔ Tasting option of your choice (Uruguayan wines)
  • ✔ Three empanadas included with options 1 and 2
  • ✔ Historic Quarter location at the wine shop
  • ✔ Coordination and support from Sacramento Adventures
  • ✔ 10% discount on in-store purchases
  • ✔ Open Monday to Friday, 11 AM to 7 PM

🌅🚤 Sunset Boat Experience

About this experience

Enjoy a relaxing boat tour along the Río de la Plata and discover Colonia from a unique perspective. Cruise through calm waters, take in sunset views, and unwind with your captain.

How it works

  • 📍 Meeting point: Historic Quarter (shared after booking)
  • 🚤 Boat tour: Navigate the Río de la Plata with captain your host and enjoy views of Colonia from the water.
  • ⏰ Departure times: 11:00am, 13:30pm, 15:30pm, 17:00pm
  • 🌅 Sunset & relax: Ideal for photos, calm waters, and a memorable end of the day.
  • ⏱️ Duration: About 1.5 hours

🛡️ Medical assistance included

USD 40 per guest

Price per person for the boat experience. Drinks are not included unless specified in your booking confirmation.

What's included

  • ✔ Scenic boat tour on the Río de la Plata
  • ✔ Captain & host
  • ✔ Life jackets and safety briefing
  • ✔ Small-group atmosphere

Book your spot

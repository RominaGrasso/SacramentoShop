🔥🚤 Asado Experience (BBQ) & Boat Tour

About this experience

Experience the true essence of Uruguay with a unique combination of gastronomy and nature. Enjoy an authentic asado prepared by your host at a partner grill restaurant. You will taste premium meats, local wine, and a relaxed atmosphere. Before or after your asado, enjoy a scenic boat tour along the Río de la Plata, discovering Colonia from a completely different perspective.

How it works

  • 📍 Meeting point: Historic Quarter (shared after booking)
  • 🔥 Asado experience: Enjoy a traditional Uruguayan BBQ prepared by your host in a local grill in the Historic Quarter with premium meats and wine.
  • 🚤 Boat tour: Navigate the Río de la Plata and enjoy stunning views of Colonia from the water with your captain.
  • 🍽 Lunch: Served at 12:30 after the first boat trip — 11:00am departure.
  • 🌅 Relax & enjoy: Perfect moment to unwind, take photos and experience the sunset.
  • 🚶 All activities are within walking distance

Standard USD 60 · Premium USD 80 per menu

Includes the BBQ menu for your group (Standard or Premium parrillero) and your choice of side dishes. Generally, the Standard BBQ is enough for about 2–3 people; Premium for about 4. Drinks and beverages are not included — you can order them on site.

Boat USD 30 per person

Charged per passenger for the boat tour, in addition to your BBQ menu order(s).

  • ✔ Local host

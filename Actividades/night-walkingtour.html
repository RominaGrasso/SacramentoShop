About this experience

When night falls, Colonia tells different stories.

Walk through the streets of the Historic Quarter with a local guide and discover the stories, secrets and legends that are part of the city’s memory.

During this 1.5-hour experience, we’ll explore some of Colonia del Sacramento’s most iconic corners while discovering stories, characters, curiosities and events that reveal a different side of the city.

An experience for travelers who want to discover Colonia beyond its postcards and uncover the stories that emerge after sunset.

Experience details

  • Duration: 1 hour 30 minutes
  • Starting times: 6:30 PM • 8:00 PM
  • Languages: Spanish, English & Portuguese
  • Candlelight experience included
  • Medical assistance included
  • Meeting point: View meeting point on Google Maps

USD 25 per person

  • Candle provided for the candlelight experience

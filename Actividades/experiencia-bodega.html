About this experience

Live an experience among vineyards and Uruguayan wines at a local winery in Colonia. Enjoy guided walks through the vines, tastings, and different pairing proposals with regional products.

🎥 Watch Reel on @_sacramentoadventures

How it works

  • 🗓️ Choose your date: Pick the day you want to visit and create your experience.
  • 🍇 Choose your option: After tapping "Create your experience", pick the proposal you like best.
  • 👥 Tell us how many people: Select how many people will enjoy the experience.
  • 🚗 Add transport if you need it: You can add round-trip transport and choose your pickup time.
  • 🍷 Enjoy the winery: Discover Uruguayan wines with guided visits and pairings, depending on your choice.
  • ✨ We coordinate the details: Our team confirms availability and final details before your visit.

What's included

  • ✔ Uruguayan wine tasting (option of your choice)
  • ✔ Guided vineyard and winery visits on selected options
  • ✔ Local cheeses and regional pairings on selected options
  • ✔ Coordination and support from Sacramento Adventures

Destilería S34 Gin Experience

Live a unique experience at an artisan distillery facing Colonia del Sacramento's iconic Plaza de Toros. Discover how gin is made, taste internationally awarded products, and choose the experience that suits you best.

  • Artisan gin distillery
  • Internationally awarded products
  • Plaza de Toros views
  • Guided by Francisco
  • Optional transfer from Colonia

From USD 45 · Gin tasting

About this experience

★★★★★ Google Reviews Trusted by travelers

A few minutes from historic downtown Colonia del Sacramento, S34 Gin is an artisan distillery recognized for the quality of its products and for earning major international awards — including recognition as the world's best gin. During your visit you will learn the history of gin, the full distillation process, and enjoy a guided experience led by Francisco, the distillery manager.

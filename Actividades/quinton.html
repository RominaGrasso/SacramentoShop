About this experience

A family winery and olive estate near Colonia is a winery just fifteen minutes from downtown Colonia del Sacramento, specialized in Malbec. The estate also houses an extra virgin olive oil factory and a gourmet restaurant where you can taste local wines in a charming atmosphere — while learning about the winery's processes and history on a guided visit.

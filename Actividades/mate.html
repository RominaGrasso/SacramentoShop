Traditional Mate Experience

Discover Uruguay’s most beloved tradition. Learn how to prepare and share mate like locals.

About this experience

Join us for a relaxed mate circle in Colonia del Sacramento. We walk you through the story of Uruguay’s national drink, how to prepare a good round, and the friendly rhythm of sharing mate with others.

What’s included

  • ✔ Introduction to mate culture and everyday etiquette
  • ✔ Guided preparation: yerba, water temperature, and sharing the gourd
  • ✔ Tasting circle with time for questions
  • ✔ Relaxed pace in small groups
  • ✔ We teach you 3 mate styles: traditional bitter, sweet, and tea mate

USD 35 per person

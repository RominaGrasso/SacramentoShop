About this experience

A guided experience that combines history, culture and gastronomy in Colonia del Sacramento. Visitors start with a walking tour of the Historic Quarter, discovering its streets, architecture, history and emblematic corners. Afterwards they continue to Charco Bistró for a complete three-course lunch with selected dishes. The proposal is organized so visitors can discover Colonia and enjoy its gastronomy without worrying about the coordination.

Watch the reel on Instagram @_sacramentoadventures

Itinerary

  • 🏛 11:00 — Walking tour of the Historic Quarter: Guided walk of about 1 hour and 15 minutes with a local guide. Meeting point: Plaza Mayor · View meeting point on Google Maps
  • 🍽 12:30 — Lunch at Charco Bistró: Three-course lunch with starter, main course, dessert and beverages. Dishes are chosen on site from the restaurant menu. Lunch begins at 12:30. End time to be confirmed.

USD 130 per person

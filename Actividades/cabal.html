About this experience

Just 10 minutes from downtown Colonia del Sacramento, a unique natural setting awaits you. Ride through a wide open countryside that leads to a beautiful beach and enjoy a horseback ride designed to connect with nature and slow down. Along the way you'll meet our loyal companions: Cacha, Mana, Negrita, Bimbo, Rosi, and Carmelito — they'll make the experience even more special. Book your spot and discover Colonia from a whole new perspective!

Private Golf Cart City Tour

Discover Colonia comfortably with a local guide aboard a private golf cart.

About this experience

★★★★★ Google Reviews Trusted by travelers

Explore Colonia del Sacramento in a comfortable and relaxed way aboard a private golf cart.

Your local guide drives throughout the entire experience, allowing you to simply sit back, enjoy the ride and discover different areas of the city.

During the tour, you will visit some of Colonia’s most iconic landmarks and scenic locations, with stops along the way to explore, take photos and learn more about the city.

The golf cart is arranged directly by Sacramento Adventures, so you don’t need to worry about renting a vehicle, driving, deposits or logistics.

You just enjoy Colonia. We take care of the rest.

No driving required. Your guide drives the golf cart throughout the entire experience.

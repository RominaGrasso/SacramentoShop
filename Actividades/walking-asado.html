🥩🏛🔥 Walking Tour + Asado Experience (BBQ)

About this experience

Discover Colonia’s UNESCO-listed Historic Quarter on foot with a professional guide, then sit down to an authentic Asado Experience (BBQ) at a local grill in the Historic Quarter with your host — premium cuts, local wine, and a relaxed atmosphere. No boat: the whole flow stays in the old town so you can focus on stories, architecture, and flavor in one seamless half-day.

How it works

  • 📍 Meeting point: Historic Quarter (exact spot shared after booking)
  • 🏛 Guided walking tour: Stroll the cobbled streets and main landmarks with your guide; choose English, Spanish, or Portuguese when you build each menu order.
  • 🔥 Asado experience: Traditional Uruguayan BBQ prepared by your host at a local grill in the Historic Quarter with premium meats and wine (menu tier and sides as you select).
  • 🍽 Lunch timing: Lunch is usually coordinated around midday at the restaurant (we confirm exact time with you after booking).
  • 🚶 Walking tour and asado are on foot in the Historic Quarter — no transfers included.

Standard USD 60 · Premium USD 80 per menu

Includes the BBQ menu for your group (Standard or Premium parrillero) and your choice of side dishes. Generally, the Standard BBQ is enough for about 2–3 people; Premium for about 4. Drinks and beverages are not included — you can order them on site.

About Sacramento Adventures

At Sacramento Adventures, we create authentic experiences for travelers who want to discover the true essence of Colonia del Sacramento and its surroundings.

From guided walking tours and full-day experiences to local gastronomy and sunset moments by the Río de la Plata, we help visitors connect with the culture, history and lifestyle of Uruguay in a genuine and memorable way.
